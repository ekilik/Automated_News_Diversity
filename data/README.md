# Data

The article texts used in this study are **not** distributed in this repository,
as NOS.nl news content is copyrighted.

The notebooks in `Scripts/` read from and write to this `data/` directory. Each
step reads the intermediate files produced by the previous step; adjust the paths
in the notebooks to your local layout.

## Gold-standard annotations

The manual annotations that serve as the ground truth (main topic, sub-topics,
actors and their function categories, and stance) are described in the paper's
appendices.
